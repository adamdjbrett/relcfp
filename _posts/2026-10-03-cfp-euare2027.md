It is with great pleasure that the European Academy of Religion announces its Tenth Annual Conference, which will take place in Münster, Germany, from May 18th to May 21st, 2027. The hosting institution will be the [University of Münster](https://www.uni-muenster.de/en/).

The topic of the 2027 conference will be: **Being Human – Religions between Peace and Conflict**.

Hosted at the new Campus of Theologies and Religious Studies, the conference brings together scholars from around the world for dialogue, exchange and collaboration.

We warmly invite you to join us for five days of inspiring keynote lectures, panel discussions, networking opportunities and academic collaboration.

## Key Dates

- **Call for Proposals:** October 1, 2026 – December 18, 2026
- **Call for Papers:** November 3, 2026 – February 21, 2027
- **Early Bird registrations:** November 3, 2026
- **Regular registrations:** February 1, 2027
- **Draft Schedule Release:** March 15, 2027
- **Schedule Alteration Requests:** March 22–27, 2027
- **Final Schedule Release:** April 19, 2027
- **Tenth Annual Conference:** May 18–21, 2027

More information: [europeanacademyofreligion.org](https://europeanacademyofreligion.org/)
